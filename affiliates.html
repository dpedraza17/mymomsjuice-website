Good to know

When do I get paid?

Commissions are ready to pay 7 days after the order is fulfilled. We send payouts through the method you chose. Before your first payout we'll ask for a W-9 tax form.

What counts as a sale?

Commission is 20% of the item total after the customer's discount, including Mama Bear Hydration. Delivery fees and tips don't earn commission.

Can I use my own code?

No. Orders placed by affiliates or their household don't earn commission.

Do I have to say I'm an affiliate?

Yes. Whenever you share your link or code, disclose that you earn a commission (for example, "#ad" or "I earn a commission if you order").
