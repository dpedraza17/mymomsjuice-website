Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information My Mom's Juice ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use mymomsjuice.com, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Delivery address (for delivery orders only)
  • Order details — items, quantities, and any note you leave for us

If you sign up for email updates through the popup on our site, we collect just your email address for that purpose.

We do not collect or store your payment card details directly — payment is handled by a secure third-party payment processor at checkout.

How we use your information

  • To prepare, fulfill, and communicate with you about your order
  • To send you an order confirmation email and, if something needs your attention, to text or call the phone number you provide
  • To calculate applicable sales tax on your order
  • To send occasional discount codes or announcements, only if you've signed up for email updates — you can ask us to stop at any time
  • To keep basic business records of past orders

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information to anyone. We do work with a small number of trusted service providers to run the business, who process data on our behalf and are contractually required to keep it secure:

  • A payment processor, to securely process payment and calculate sales tax
  • A database hosting provider, to securely store order and customer records
  • An email delivery service, to send our order confirmation emails

Each of these providers has its own privacy and security practices governing how they handle data on our behalf.

Cookies and site technology

Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical information like your IP address as part of serving that content — this is standard for most websites and outside our control. We don't use advertising trackers or sell browsing data to third parties.

How long we keep your information

We keep order and customer records for as long as reasonably needed for business, tax, and recordkeeping purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or unsubscribe from email updates, at any time by contacting us using the information below.

Children's privacy

Our site and services are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
