Affiliate Program Terms

Last updated November 15, 2026

These terms govern your participation in the My Mom's Juice affiliate program (the "Program"). By applying, you agree to them. "We," "us," and "My Mom's Juice" mean the business operating mymomsjuice.com.

1. Joining

You must be at least 18 and able to receive payments in the United States. We review every application and can approve or decline it at our discretion. You choose a code when you apply; we may change a code that is misleading or already in use.

2. How you earn

  • You earn a commission (currently 20%, unless we tell you a different rate in writing) on the item subtotal after any discount of qualifying orders, including Mama Bear Hydration products. Delivery fees, tips, and taxes do not earn commission.
  • A qualifying order comes from a new customer who orders through your link or uses your code. You earn on that customer's first three orders placed within 90 days of their first order, even if they don't use your link or code again.
  • If a customer visits more than one affiliate's link, the most recent link within 30 days gets credit, and a creator code entered at checkout takes priority over a link. Once a customer is credited to an affiliate, they stay with that affiliate for the first-three-orders window.
  • Customers who have ordered from us before do not qualify.

3. Customer discount

Your code gives a new customer 10% off the items in their first order. The code cannot be combined with other promo codes and does not apply to delivery fees or tips. We may change or end the discount at any time.

4. Payment

  • A commission becomes payable 7 days after the order's fulfillment date. We pay by the method you provided (such as Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, or Cash App).
  • All sales are final and we do not offer refunds. A commission is not earned on any order that is canceled, disputed, charged back, or refunded. If a commission was already paid on an order that is later disputed or refunded, we may deduct it from future payouts.
  • Before your first payout you must provide a completed IRS Form W-9. We may withhold payment until we have it.

5. Taxes

You are an independent contractor, not an employee, and you are responsible for the taxes on what you earn. We may be required to report your earnings to the IRS (for example on Form 1099) if they reach the legal threshold.

6. What's not allowed

  • Ordering through your own link or code, or having family, household members, or friends place orders on your behalf to earn commission.
  • Fake, duplicate, or fraudulent orders, or any attempt to manipulate tracking.
  • Misleading claims about My Mom's Juice, including health claims we haven't made, or false statements about prices or discounts.
  • Paid advertising that bids on our brand name or impersonates our website, and spam or unsolicited messages.

Commissions earned through prohibited conduct are void, and we may remove you from the Program.

7. Disclose your relationship

Whenever you share your link or code, you must clearly disclose that you earn a commission (for example, "#ad" or "I earn a commission if you order"). The disclosure must be easy to see and understand, in the same format as your post (spoken in videos, written in captions). This is required by U.S. Federal Trade Commission rules.

8. Our content

You may use our name, logo, and photos only to promote My Mom's Juice in ways that follow these terms. You may not imply that we endorse you beyond the Program, and you must stop using our materials if we ask.

9. Changes and ending

We may change these terms, commission rates, or the Program at any time, and we will post updates here. You may leave at any time. We may suspend or end your participation at any time; commissions already earned and payable on valid orders will still be paid.
